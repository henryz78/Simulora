## 3. Scope and findings

The library covers environmental and UI audio, adaptive/spatial sound, weather/time, dynamic maps and backgrounds, timelines, stateful character motion, Three.js/WebGL/WebGPU, MapLibre/Globe, Rive/Lottie, particles, shaders and procedural/audio-reactive systems.

High-value synthesis: immersion is strongest when sound, motion, maps, text and notifications read the same world state; feedback operates at immediate, event and ambient time scales; important interactions leave causal “aftereffects”; quiet mode, reduced motion and graceful degradation are essential.

## 4. Evidence and limitations

Evidence combines secondary research, official documentation, public demos and open-source/tool references. Tool or source-code licensing does not license demo media, datasets or community assets. Performance, device coverage, accessibility, integration complexity and current commercial terms remain selection-specific.
