## 3. Scope and method

Public first-person posts, reviews, issue discussions and related sources were structured across five passes. Coverage includes continuity, Memory, character behavior, agency, creator/player workflows, game loops, payment, governance, migration, accessibility, UI continuity and model trade-offs. Public-source selection and regional/segment gaps remain.

## 5. Do-not-repeat boundary

- Do not run another broad public-source user survey for completeness.
- Do not convert qualitative frequency into market share or universal priority.
- Do not flatten contradictory modes into one user archetype.
- Keep evidence references and source scope attached to every later claim.

## 6. Gaps and merge decision

Nontechnical long-horizon operators, very large creator libraries, casual game-loop preference, independent-character safety and deeper regional variation remain bounded P1 gaps. None is P0. The package supports the need layer and is integrated through `MERGE-01` to `MERGE-15` as applicable.
