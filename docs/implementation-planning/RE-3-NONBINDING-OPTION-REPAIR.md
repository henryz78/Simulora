## Root cause / chosen boundary

The subject/authority-verb heuristic interpreted
`If the test holds, the keeper can decide; until then, invite nothing.`
as an authored user decision. It actually leaves the choice to the user.

In server-bound non-user Character narrative only, recognize **bare `can decide`
at a clause end**, with an existing user subject and supported English spelling.
For guard inspection, neutralize only this verb, retaining the subject and every
other claim. Do not ignore a whole sentence, conditional, quotation or modal.
The original narrative remains unchanged in generation/proposal evidence.

`has decided`, `will decide`, `can decide to approve`, `can decide and agrees`,
and protected claims before/after the option remain blocked. User/Character name
collisions, unsupported scripts, disclosure fences, canonical statements and
provenance retain their prior restrictions. This deliberately does not attempt
general grammar understanding; other unrecognized harmless options may still
fail closed.
