## Product direction boundary

inhabit it through a Character while other Characters and the world respond
with causal continuity. The current first-fact/first-Character/effect-envelope
implementation is an approved intermediate spine, not the final product
ceiling. Future work must test whether the experience expands toward that goal
without weakening authority, truth, Recovery or confirmation semantics.
