## Audit rules

1. A discovered control is not marked triggered merely because its label or tooltip was read.
2. A failed click, disabled state, permission wall or stale-session error is recorded under “Unverified / failure reason” with the observed UI result.
3. Final audit must add every newly discovered route and every nested modal/drawer as its own row or explicit sub-control list.
4. Browser-only safety confirmations, membership walls, real-money payment and API-key submission remain explicit boundary states rather than reasons to infer behavior.
