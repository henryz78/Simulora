# Economy System

Status: `PARTIAL / TESTED`

Wallet App exposes currency, balance and timestamped transactions. AI/Event Delta can add rewards or spend. Shop purchases are Turn actions: repeated ration clicks charged two ledger lines, updated stock, Inventory and Story. Credits/Zaps are separate account-level AI usage currency and are not rewound.

Evidence: EVD-0016, EVD-0030, EVD-0043.
