## 1. Evidence hierarchy

When sources differ, use this order to decide what a statement can support:

1. **User-need evidence** may support why a problem deserves product attention, within its qualitative sample limits.
2. **Direct competitor observation** may support only what WorldOS did in the tested identity, object, state and time window.
3. **Creator templates and original content** may support mechanism coverage, scenario diversity and test ideas; they do not establish demand.
4. **Asset and immersion libraries** may support production feasibility, implementation options and later cost/risk assessment; they do not establish requirements.
5. **Brand exploration** may support positioning alternatives and naming vocabulary; it does not establish the final brand.

No lower layer may override a higher layer merely because it is more concrete or already contains assets/code.
