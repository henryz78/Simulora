Do not clear every asset or every name before selection.

### 11. What evidence can Product Definition use?

Use the following order:

1. User Needs and their evidence references for problem and audience rationale.
2. The Conflict Register for choices that must be explicit.
3. WorldOS evidence for bounded observed behavior, risk and counterexample—not requirements.
4. Creator templates and original content for scenario/mechanism tests.
5. Asset and immersion packages for later feasibility/cost options.
6. Brand exploration only after capability and positioning are chosen.

For every later product decision, record `user evidence`, `reference evidence`, `our original decision`, `reason` and `acceptance test`.

### 12. How do we prevent repeated research and competitor copying?

Keep all original packages immutable; route through this index; retain provenance labels; apply the P0-only research gate; reject “WorldOS has it” as sufficient rationale; keep reference code/assets/content outside product implementation until independently selected; and require an original decision with user rationale and acceptance test. Never convert parity, templates or catalogue availability into a backlog.
