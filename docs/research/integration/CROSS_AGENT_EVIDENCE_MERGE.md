# Cross-Agent Evidence Merge

Status: `COMPLETE / FROZEN WITH INTEGRATED RESEARCH V1`

This synthesis relates findings without erasing differences in audience, method, identity, time or evidence quality. Labels mean:

- `SUPPORT` — independent sources support the same bounded conclusion.
- `COMPLEMENT` — sources address different layers that can be combined.
- `CONFLICT` — needs or observations pull in incompatible directions.
- `CONDITIONAL` — a conclusion applies only to a segment, mode or stated condition.
- `UNKNOWN` — current evidence cannot settle the question.

## 2. High-confidence synthesis for Product Definition

The following statements have enough cross-source support to become **inputs** to Original Product Principles. They are not principles or requirements yet.

1. Continuity is a system-wide promise, not one Memory field.
2. User control includes inspection, correction, recovery, migration and a clear participation contract.
3. World and character change should be causal, legible and mode-appropriate.
4. Creator power and onboarding simplicity are distinct needs that require scope or progressive disclosure decisions.
5. Game mechanics are valuable for a segment, not universal proof of product category.
6. Visual and sensory ambition is valuable only when it preserves performance, accessibility and authoritative state continuity.
7. Safety, privacy, attribution and exit rights belong in the core promise.
8. Model quality is a multi-objective trade-off, not a single leaderboard score.

## 3. Findings intentionally not unified

The merge does not choose:

- companion, sandbox, co-writing or game-loop as the initial mode;
- system-led progression or player-led progression;
- a fixed level of character independence;
- a launch creator complexity tier;
- a Memory retention/budget policy;
- a model, technical stack, data model or pricing scheme;
- a visual style, asset set, product name or brand position.

These are genuine product choices. Their evidence is recorded in the [Conflict Register](CONFLICT_REGISTER.md), not hidden behind a false consensus.

## 4. Provenance rules carried forward

- WorldOS `VERIFIED` remains bounded to its documented identity, object, URL, time and state.
- `TESTED`, `PARTIAL`, `REPORT_ONLY` and `CONTRADICTORY_SNAPSHOT` are never silently upgraded.
- Public qualitative user evidence supports needs and tensions, not market size or universal preference.
- Third-party IP and official media links are `REF-ONLY`; abstraction of mechanism never authorizes copying expression.
- Tool/library code licences do not automatically cover demo media, datasets or community assets.
- Original AI visuals remain candidate assets until human similarity, brand and rights review.
