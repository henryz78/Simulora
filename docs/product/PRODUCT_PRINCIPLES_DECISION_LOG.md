## Directional decisions made

| Decision | Direction chosen | Why | Principles |
|---|---|---|---|
| Core experience | Prioritize a coherent, recoverable, user-governed living world over infinite content generation | Continuity, state, recovery and agency are the strongest cross-source synthesis; infinite generation without structure is a documented failure mode | 1, 3, 5 |
| AI initiative | Use a separate initiative/authority dimension: Direct, Guided or World-active; preserve user authority over avatar, resources and irreversible commitments | Participation evidence is genuinely conditional, not universal; users need both momentum and control | 2, 4 |
| Sandbox vs game loop | Use a separate world-structure dimension: Open-ended or Goal-framed; launch with Guided + Open-ended and keep goal framing optional | N50/N51 show a real segment conflict; templates support goals but do not prove a universal game category | 2, 3 |
| Continuity vs creativity | Preserve authoritative, user-understandable continuity while enabling branches, correction and experimentation | Users need stable identity and recoverability without giving up creative freedom | 1, 5, 7 |
| Character autonomy | Permit grounded disagreement and refusal without allowing character control of the user | Independent stance improves reciprocity; user-avatar authority and safety are non-negotiable | 4, 10 |
| Automation | Automate low-risk assistance, but make consequential changes observable, attributable and recoverable | Memory/state automation is necessary; invisible irreversible change destroys trust | 5 |
| Creator experience | Use progressive disclosure: fast playable entry plus advanced, testable controls | Creator power and onboarding simplicity are both real needs | 6 |
| Persistence and exit | Treat portability, recovery and dignified exit as product behavior, not optional goodwill | Migration, shutdown and ownership evidence is high-impact and under-served | 1, 7, 10 |
| Immersion | Make sensory and dynamic layers enhance state understanding, never gate basic use | Immersion research is complementary; performance/accessibility and session reliability remain foundational | 8 |
| Model strategy | Make the product absorb model variance instead of promising one model can satisfy every quality dimension | Model quality is multi-objective and changes over time; product structure must carry reliability | 1, 5, 9 |
| Clean-room posture | Build original interaction language, IA, visual system, positioning and implementation; use references only for bounded evidence/mechanism | WorldOS and all other packages are reference layers, not design specifications | Clean-room rules A–E |

| Final brand and naming | Capability promise must precede brand commitment | Product Positioning + formal clearance |
