# Target Users and Jobs-to-be-Done

Status: `TARGET USERS / JTBD V1: FROZEN AFTER RE-AUDIT`

The target definition follows the player-first positioning in [PRODUCT_POSITIONING.md](PRODUCT_POSITIONING.md). Jobs describe what users are trying to accomplish, not the features used to accomplish it.

## Target hierarchy

| Segment | Role in launch | What success feels like |
|---|---|---|
| Continuity-seeking world participant | **Primary** | “I can return to a world that knows what matters, choose how much I lead, and see my actions matter.” |
| Advanced solo world creator | Secondary | “I can shape a world without building a fragile prompt system, test it, and hand someone a playable experience.” |
| Guided newcomer / low-technical player | Secondary | “I can begin immediately and understand what I can do without learning hidden authoring rules.” |

Product Definition V1 is adult-only. Migration and recovery are cross-cutting situations for the primary participant, not a separate audience segment.

## Core JTBD

### JTBD-01 — Resume a meaningful world without rebuilding context

**When:** I return after hours, days or many sessions.

**I want to:** understand where the world, my relationships and my open threads stand, and continue without restating the canon or guessing whether my history survived.

**User:** Primary continuity-seeking world participant.

**Need IDs / evidence:** N01, N02, N03, N09, N21, N23, N43, N46; E01, E02, E07, E22, E67, E71, E76.

**Product Principles:** 1 Continuity; 5 Observable automation; 7 Ownership/exit; 9 Model-absorbed reliability.

**Success condition:** A returning user can identify current world state, recent meaningful changes, open threads and safe next actions without manual reconstruction; a short interruption or device change does not feel like permanent loss.

**Unmet need:** Existing systems often preserve a transcript but lose usable facts, relationship context, time/location continuity or recovery clarity.

### JTBD-02 — Decide how much the world and AI should lead

**When:** I enter a world with a particular energy level, time budget or creative intention.

**I want to:** choose whether I directly author my actions, accept guided suggestions or let bounded background activity continue, without the system taking over my character or stalling when I want momentum.

**User:** Primary participant; guided newcomer and high-control co-writer as secondary contexts.

**Need IDs / evidence:** N20, N24, N29, N30, N35, N37, N50, N51, N52; E77, E78, E79.

**Product Principles:** 2 Participation contract; 4 Character stance; 10 Governance and reciprocity.

**Success condition:** The user can separately identify the active initiative contract (Direct, Guided or World-active) and world structure (Open-ended or Goal-framed), change allowed settings, reject consequential proposals and verify that the AI has not silently authored their avatar or irreversible commitments.

**Unmet need:** Current products treat “more AI initiative” as universally good, leaving passive users bored and high-control users feeling overridden.

### JTBD-03 — Keep facts, relationships and consequences coherent without babysitting prompts

**When:** A world accumulates characters, locations, resources, secrets and changing relationships.

**I want to:** trust that important facts are recalled in the right context, that characters know only what they should, and that actions change the world for understandable reasons.

**User:** Primary participant; advanced solo creator as secondary.

**Need IDs / evidence:** N03–N12, N15, N20, N25, N44, N48; E03–E08, E11, E14–E16, E24–E26, E48, E60, E68, E74.

**Product Principles:** 1 Continuity; 3 Causal consequences; 4 Character stance; 5 Observable automation; 9 Model reliability.

**Success condition:** A user can correct a wrong fact, understand why a state or relationship changed, and continue without maintaining a fragile stack of prompts, keywords or external notes.

**Unmet need:** Existing systems push memory selection, state-machine logic and continuity repair onto users while still failing to preserve the right context.

### JTBD-04 — Shape a playable personal world without becoming a systems engineer

**When:** I have a premise, character or relationship idea I want to explore repeatedly.

**I want to:** establish the important facts and boundaries, preview the result, and begin playing with confidence; if I become more advanced, I want deeper control without being forced to learn it up front.

**User:** Primary participant as a light creator; advanced solo world creator as secondary.

**Need IDs / evidence:** N14–N17, N27–N28, N31, N33, N39, N44; T01–T37 as mechanism reference; E13–E16, E19–E21, E39, E62, E67, E77.

**Product Principles:** 6 Progressive creator power; 7 Ownership; 10 Governance.

**Success condition:** A new user can create or adapt a small world that is playable out of the box. Advanced preview, testing and packaging are conditional on the corresponding MVP `SHOULD` capabilities and must not be required for first success.

**Unmet need:** Existing creator workflows either hide the controls that matter or expose a brittle expert stack that transfers maintenance to the author and player.

### JTBD-05 — Protect, recover and carry forward long-term investment

**When:** The product, model, device, payment status or platform changes—or I want to experiment safely.

**I want to:** inspect what I have, preserve the original, branch or restore a safe version, export the important parts and continue elsewhere if necessary.

**User:** Primary participant in recovery, service-change or migration situations.

**Need IDs / evidence:** N13, N22–N23, N28–N29, N34, N37, N41–N47; E19, E22, E41, E43, E45, E58–E59, E62, E64–E68, E70–E72.

**Product Principles:** 1 Continuity; 5 Recoverable automation; 7 Ownership/exit; 10 Governance.

**Success condition:** The user can tell what is current, what is private/shared, what will be preserved, what a branch or rewind changes, and how to recover or export without losing the original.

**Unmet need:** Export is often a dead transcript, account or model changes silently damage relationships, and sync or moderation failures are indistinguishable from permanent loss.

## JTBD prioritization rule

JTBD-01 through JTBD-03 are the launch spine. JTBD-04 makes the product personally shapeable without changing its player-first priority. JTBD-05 is a trust and continuity requirement that protects every other job over time.

No JTBD is justified by WorldOS parity, template count, asset availability, immersion demos or brand exploration alone.
